Two ways to help, one way to reach them

CBETA runs on a foundation's budget, not a company's, and it keeps everything it produces free. That makes it unusually dependent on people who use the archive choosing to give something back — money or time.

Give money

CBETA accepts donations by credit card, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, ATM transfer, postal transfer and cash, through the "支持我們" (support us) section of their site. If you're donating from outside Taiwan, look for the English-language sponsorship page linked from there — it's currently the one part of the site built for a non-Chinese reader.

Give time or code

CBETA publishes its own tooling and text data openly on GitHub. If you write software, that's the way in — issues and pull requests reach the same team that builds CBETA Online and CBReader, not a separate "developer relations" layer.
