Notes: what shipped with the theme

Zen, the Astro theme this site is built on, is a blog theme. A blog theme ships with sample posts. This page doesn't hide that — it's the theme's own placeholder content, word for word, still sitting where the template put it.

Why these are still here

A real deployment deletes these before going live. Leaving them in is the point: it's evidence that nothing about the front-end is hand-forged from scratch — it's a template, running, with CBETA content poured into its actual pages and the template's own scaffolding left standing everywhere else. See also the install instructions in the footer of every page.
